The 5 Most Common Job Scam Patterns in India
Registration Fee Scams ask you to pay ₹500–₹5,000 to "register" before getting hired. No legitimate employer charges candidates. Guaranteed Job Scams promise placement without any interview — real jobs always have a selection process. Work From Home Scams promise unrealistic earnings like ₹50,000/month for simple data entry. Impersonation Scams use company names like TCS or Infosys but contact you via WhatsApp personal numbers. Data Harvesting Scams ask for your Aadhaar, PAN, or bank details before you've even been interviewed.

How to Verify a Job Offer is Legitimate
Always verify the company exists by searching on Google and checking their official website. Legitimate HR emails come from company domains — not Gmail or Yahoo. Call the company's official number (from their website, not the one in the message) to confirm the offer. Never share Aadhaar, PAN, or bank details before signing a verified offer letter. If an offer arrives on WhatsApp from an unknown number claiming to be a major company, treat it as suspicious.

WhatsApp & Telegram Scam Signals
Messages forwarded many times are often scams. Urgency language like "apply within 24 hours" or "limited seats" is a pressure tactic. Google Form links for job applications are a major red flag — real companies use official portals. Messages in poor English with spelling mistakes often come from scammers operating at scale. "Join our premium group" or "pay to unlock the full offer" are instant disqualifiers.

What to Do If You've Been Scammed
File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in immediately — India's national cybercrime portal. Call the Cyber Crime Helpline at 1930. Report the scam on ScamShield so others in the community are warned. Block the sender on WhatsApp and report the number. If you've shared bank details, contact your bank immediately to freeze any suspicious transactions. Document all messages and payment receipts before reporting.
